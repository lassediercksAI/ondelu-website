How it works

Real physics.
Real presence.

Ondelu runs a full fluid simulation on your GPU — the same mathematics that governs real liquids, executed 120 times per second.

👆

Touch-reactive

Drag to stir. Tap for a radial ripple. The fluid responds to every gesture with physical momentum.

📱

Motion-aware

Tilt your phone and pigments shift under gravity. Lay it flat and the fluid settles into silence.

✨

Rheoscopic shimmer

Millions of microscopic flakes orient in the flow, catching light like real pearlescent ink.

⚡️

Metal-powered

Five compute shader passes per frame. Navier-Stokes pressure solving at full display resolution.

🔇

Quietly alive

When still, energy fades naturally. When you move, the fluid wakes up. No jarring transitions.

🌑

Pure dark

No UI chrome. No distractions. Just the fluid and the glow of your screen at night.
